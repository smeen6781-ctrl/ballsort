How to Play Ball Sort Puzzle

Ball Sort Puzzle is a relaxing color-sorting game. Your goal is simple: move the balls between tubes until every tube holds balls of just one color. It is easy to learn, but the later levels need real planning.

Play Now

The goal

A level is complete when each tube is either empty or completely filled with balls of the same color. When a tube is fully sorted, its border turns green.

The basic rules

  1. Tap a tube to pick up the ball on top of it. The tube lifts and glows yellow to show it is selected.
  2. Tap another tube to move the ball there. Tap the same tube again to cancel your selection.
  3. A ball can only be placed on an empty tube or on a ball of the same color.
  4. Each tube holds a limited number of balls. A full tube cannot take any more.
  5. If several balls of the same color sit together on top of a tube, they move together, as long as there is room in the target tube.

The buttons

  • Restart: reshuffles the current level and starts it again.
  • Undo: takes back your last move. You can use it as many times as you like, so experiment freely.
  • Add Tube: adds one extra empty tube when you feel stuck. You can add tubes up to a maximum of 12.
  • Next: skips to the next level.
  • Sound and Music: switch the sound effects and background music on or off.

Scoring

  • You earn 10 points for every ball you move.
  • When you finish a level, you get 100 points multiplied by the level number.
  • You also get a speed bonus of up to 100 points. It shrinks by 2 points for every move you make, so fewer moves means a bigger bonus.

How levels get harder

The first levels use just a few colors and 4 balls per tube. As you progress, the game adds more colors and taller tubes that hold 5, 6 and eventually 7 balls. Every level always has two empty tubes at the start, and every level the game creates can be solved.

Tips and strategy

  • Look before you move. Check which colors are buried at the bottom of each tube before you start. Those are the balls you will need to reach.
  • Free up an empty tube early. An empty tube is your most powerful tool, because any ball can go into it.
  • Build on same-colored balls. If a color is already on top of a tube, gather the other balls of that color onto it.
  • Do not fill your empty tubes too fast. Keep at least one empty tube free as long as you can.
  • Work from the top down. Uncover the colors that are blocking others first, then sort the rest.
  • Use Undo to explore. If a plan leads to a dead end, take the move back and try another route.
  • Stuck? Use Add Tube for extra room, or Restart for a fresh arrangement.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?
Yes. Ball Sort Puzzle is free to play in your browser, with no download and no sign-up.

Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The game is designed for phone screens and also works on tablets and computers.

Is my progress saved?
No. Your level and score live in your browser while you play and are reset when you close or reload the page.

Why is there no sound at first?
Browsers only allow sound after you tap the screen once. Tap anywhere, and the sound and music will start.
