About Ball Sort Puzzle

Ball Sort Puzzle is a free color-sorting puzzle game that you can play right in your web browser. There is nothing to download or install, and you do not need an account. Open the page, tap a tube, and start sorting.

Why we made it

We wanted a simple, calming puzzle that anyone can pick up in a few seconds, and that still gets interesting as the levels grow. Ball Sort Puzzle is made for short breaks, commutes and quiet moments, and it works well on phones as well as computers.

What you will find in the game

  • Endless levels that get gradually harder, with more colors and taller tubes.
  • Levels that are checked so that every one of them can be solved.
  • Unlimited undo, so you can try ideas without worry.
  • An extra tube option when you need more room.
  • Smooth animations, sound effects and relaxing background music, which you can turn off at any time.

New to the game?
